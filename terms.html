What Faithward is

Faithward is a Christian devotional companion written from an evangelical, Protestant perspective. It offers Bible reading, devotionals, prayer tools, guidance for hard moments and a moderated community.

Not professional advice

Faithward is not a substitute for a pastor, counselor, doctor, lawyer or emergency service. Fasting guidance is spiritual, not medical; talk to a doctor before fasting from food, especially if you are pregnant, have a medical condition or take medication. If you are in crisis, call or text 988 in the U.S. If you are in danger, call 911.

Your account

You can use most of Faithward without an account. If you create one, keep your password safe and give accurate information. You may delete your account at any time in Profile.

Community rules

  • You must be 13 or older to use Connect.
  • Speak the truth in love. No insults, harassment, hate, threats, sexual content, spam, selling or recruiting.
  • No shaming and no spiritual pressure. Never pressure anyone to attend, give, serve or forgive on a timeline.
  • Respect privacy. Do not share someone else's story or details without permission.
  • Faithward has zero tolerance for objectionable content and abusive users. You must agree to the Community Guidelines before posting.
  • Anonymous posts hide your name from other members, but Faithward moderators can see which account posted so we can keep people safe. No app can promise complete anonymity.
  • You can report any post and block any person. We review reports within 24 hours, remove content that breaks these rules, and may pause or permanently stop accounts from posting. Posts reported by several people are hidden automatically.
  • Faithward is not an emergency service. If you or someone else is in danger, call or text 988, or call 911.

Your content

You own what you write. You give Faithward permission to store and display it only as needed to run the app, for example syncing your private entries to your devices or showing your community posts to other members.

Devotionals, reflections, prayers, artwork and design written or made by Faithward are labeled in the app and are owned by Faithward. Please do not copy them for commercial use without permission.

Subscriptions and free trial

Faithward is a paid app. A subscription costs $6.99 per month or $39.99 per year (U.S. prices) and includes every feature. New subscribers get a 7-day free trial. There is no weekly plan. Subscriptions are not available yet, and nothing is charged today.

Some things are always free without a subscription: crisis help, opening a Blessing Card someone sends you, our Privacy Policy and Terms, and deleting your account.

At launch, subscriptions will be sold through the Apple App Store and charged to your Apple account. If you don't cancel during the free trial, your subscription starts when the trial ends. Subscriptions renew automatically unless you cancel at least 24 hours before the end of the current period. You can manage or cancel anytime in your Apple account settings. Refunds are handled by Apple.
