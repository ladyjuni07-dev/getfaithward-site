What Even Here is

Even Here is a Christian devotional companion written from an evangelical, Protestant perspective. It offers Bible reading, devotionals, prayer tools, fasting guidance and help for hard moments. Even Here is run by its owner in Colorado Springs, Colorado ("Even Here," "we" or "us").

Not professional advice

Even Here is not a substitute for a pastor, counselor, doctor, lawyer or emergency service. Fasting guidance is spiritual, not medical; talk to a doctor before fasting from food, especially if you are pregnant, have a medical condition or take medication. If you are in crisis, call or text 988 in the U.S. If you are in danger, call 911.

Subscriptions and free trial

Even Here has a free plan and a paid membership. The free plan includes the Bible, Scripture for what you're going through with day 1 of each walk, Temptation SOS, Peace Mode, your private journal and saved verses, and 5 Blessing Cards each calendar month for everyday moments and milestones. Holiday cards are part of membership, or anyone can unlock them with the one-time Holiday Pack. Membership costs $7.99 per month or $49.99 per year (U.S. prices) and unlocks everything, including unlimited Blessing Cards. New members get a 2-week free trial. There is no weekly plan.

Some things are always free without a subscription: crisis help, opening a Blessing Card someone sends you, our Privacy Policy and Terms, and deleting your account. Unused free cards don't carry over to the next month.

Card packs are one-time purchases, not subscriptions. The 10 Blessing Card pack ($1.99) adds 10 cards that never expire, used only after your 5 free cards for the month. The Holiday Pack ($2.99) unlocks every holiday card for your account for as long as Even Here offers them. You don't need an account to buy. The Holiday Pack is tied to your Apple ID, so Restore purchases brings it back on a new phone. Cards from 10-card packs are kept on your Even Here account if you're signed in; without an account they stay on that phone and can be lost if you delete the app or change phones.

Membership and card packs are sold through the Apple App Store and charged to your Apple account. You don't need an Even Here account to subscribe; your membership follows your Apple ID. If you don't cancel during the free trial, your subscription starts when the trial ends. Subscriptions renew automatically unless you cancel at least 24 hours before the end of the current period. You can manage or cancel anytime in your Apple account settings. Refunds are handled by Apple under its own refund rules, so Even Here can't issue or deny refunds. To ask Apple for one, sign in at reportaproblem.apple.com and choose Request a refund. Canceling stops future renewals but doesn't refund the current period.
