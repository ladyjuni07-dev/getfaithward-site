Privacy Policy

Effective November 8, 2026

Even Here is a Christian Bible and devotional app. Many of the things you write in it are deeply personal, so we built it to collect as little as possible and to keep your private words private. This policy explains what we collect, why, and the choices you have.

The short version

  • You can use Even Here without an account. Without one, everything you save stays on your device.
  • Nothing you type in Even Here is sent to AI. That includes your prayers, journal entries, notes, check-ins, healing reflections and the words you type in "What are you facing?".
  • We do not sell your data, show ads, or use third-party analytics or tracking.
  • You can delete your account and its data at any time from Profile in the app.

Information you give us

  • Account (optional): if you sign up with email, your email address and a password, handled by our authentication provider (Supabase). We never see your password. If you use Sign in with Apple, Apple shares your name and an email address with us. You can choose Hide My Email, and Apple will give us a private relay address instead.
  • Your Even Here content: saved verses, prayers, journal entries, Scripture notes, fasts, plans, check-ins, healing reflections and settings. If you are signed in, these are stored in our database so they sync across your devices, protected so that only your account can read or change them.
  • Connect (not available yet): the Prayer Wall, groups and Friends are not turned on in the current app. If we turn them on, they would use your community name, posts, replies, prayer responses, group memberships, friends, blocks and reports. Posts and replies would be visible to other members by design, and moderators could review reported content. We will update this policy before they are turned on.
  • Blessing Cards: when you send a card, its words (names, message, verse choice and prayer) are saved under a random private code so the link stays short. Anyone with the link can open that card, but cards cannot be searched or listed. The card is not connected to your account. Card replies: the person who opens a card can choose to send one reply from a fixed list (for example "Amen" or two hearts). Nothing typed is sent. The reply is saved with the card, and only the sender can see it, using a private key kept with the sender's own copy of the card. We store only a scrambled version (a hash) of that key. We never record whether, when or how often a card was opened.
  • Waitlist: if you join our launch waitlist on our website, your email address, used only to tell you when Even Here is available.

"What are you facing?" and AI

When you type in "What are you facing?", the iPhone app matches your words to topics from the Even Here library on your phone. What you type is not sent to us, to AI, or anywhere else.

The current iPhone app has no live AI features. If we add an AI feature later, such as Smart matching, it will be off until you choose to allow it, and we will update this policy first.

AI never writes Scripture in Even Here. Verses always come from the King James Version or World English Bible.

How Even Here uses AI

  • Scripture: never written by AI. Every verse comes from the King James Version or World English Bible.
  • Reflections, prayers, studies and summaries marked "Written by Even Here" were drafted with help from AI writing tools, then reviewed and edited by the Even Here team.
  • Artwork: Selah, the Blessing Card illustrations and backgrounds were made with AI image tools and chosen by the Even Here team.
  • Live AI: none in the current iPhone app. Nothing you type is sent to AI.
  • Your private words (prayers, journals, notes, check-ins and healing entries) are never sent to AI and never used to train AI.

Information we do not collect

  • We do not collect your location, contacts, photos or microphone audio. Even Here never records audio. If you use your keyboard's dictation, that is handled by your device.
  • We do not use advertising identifiers, ad networks, or third-party analytics.
  • Reminders you set, such as fasting or check-in reminders, are scheduled on your phone. We don't receive them.

How we use information

  • To run Even Here: saving and syncing your content, signing you in, and checking App Store purchases.
  • To keep any future community features safe: reviewing reports, hiding harmful posts, and honoring blocks.
  • To contact you about your account, or about launch if you joined the waitlist.
  • We never use your private content to train AI models.

Who we share with

We share information only with service providers that help run Even Here, under their own privacy and security terms: our database and authentication provider (Supabase), our website hosting provider, Apple when you sign in with Apple, download the app, or buy a membership or card pack, and RevenueCat, which checks App Store purchases for us. RevenueCat receives your purchase history and a random app ID (your account ID if you're signed in), never your journal, prayers or other entries. We may also disclose information if the law requires it or to protect someone's safety.

Safety

If you type words that suggest you may be in crisis or in danger, Even Here shows help such as the 988 Suicide & Crisis Lifeline or the National Domestic Violence Hotline. This check happens on your device. We are not notified, and we do not contact anyone.

Your choices

  • Use Even Here without an account, so your data stays on your device.
  • Turn daily check-ins and reminders off in Profile.
  • Edit or delete individual entries at any time.
  • Delete your account in Profile. This permanently removes your account and the content stored with it.
  • To be removed from the waitlist, or to ask about your data, contact us using the details below.

Children

Even Here is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. Accounts are for people 13 and older. Before an account is created on iPhone, we may ask Apple for an age range (for example, under 13, 13 to 17, or 18 and over) where the law requires it. Apple shares only the range, never a birthday, and we keep only a note on your device that the check passed. You can use Even Here without an account at any age. If you believe a child has given us information, contact us and we will delete it.

Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections, and signed-in content is protected by database access rules so that each account can reach only its own private data. No system is perfectly secure, but we work to protect what you trust us with.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the effective date and, for important changes, let you know in the app.

Questions

Reach us at support@getevenhere.com.
