Healing from Church Hurt

What people did is not who God is.

Many people are hurt by the church while they're trying to get closer to God. Even Here was made with them in mind: no shame, no pressure to go back, and Scripture that tells the truth about God.

"I wanted to build this app because so many people have church hurt, and most times church people hurt those who are trying to get closer to God."

Even Here's founder
  • A 7-day start, then a gentle healing step every day for as long as you need.
  • "What was said to you": name the words, and hear what God actually says.
  • Safety help first. If someone at home is hurting you and you were told to tolerate it, Even Here shows the hotline and the truth: love does not harm.
  • A quick Exit button on every healing screen, and a way to take a break from community.

Questions

Good to know

When will Even Here be available?

Even Here is in private testing now and coming to iPhone. Join the waitlist and we'll email you once on launch day.

How much does Even Here cost?

Even Here has a free plan, and membership is $7.99 a month or $49.99 a year with a 7-day free trial. Free includes the Bible, Scripture for what you're going through, Temptation SOS, Peace Mode, a private journal and 5 Blessing Cards every month. Membership unlocks everything else, and there's no weekly plan.

Some things are always free: crisis help, opening a Blessing Card someone sends you, our Privacy Policy and Terms, and deleting your account.

What are Blessing Cards?

Blessing Cards are illustrated cards that let someone know you're praying for them, with a verse, a short prayer and your own message. The person opens it from a link, with no app or account needed.

How do I cancel?

Subscriptions are handled by Apple. Cancel anytime in your iPhone's Settings under your Apple Account, then Subscriptions. Cancel at least 24 hours before your trial or billing period ends to avoid the next charge. Even Here isn't in the App Store yet, so nothing is charged today.

Which Bible translations are included?

The King James Version and the World English Bible, both in the public domain. Every verse is the real text, never written or changed by AI.

Does AI read my prayers or journal?

No. Prayers, journal entries, notes, check-ins and healing reflections are never sent to AI. Only the words you type into "What are you facing?" are used to suggest a matching situation, and they aren't stored.

Who is Even Here for?

Evangelical and Protestant Christians, new believers, and anyone walking back toward God, including people who have been hurt by church.

Is Even Here a replacement for counseling or a church?

No. Even Here is a devotional companion. It isn't therapy, medical advice or emergency help. If you're in crisis, call or text 988 in the U.S., or call 911.

Do I need an account?

No. You can start right away and everything stays on your phone. Sign in if you want your saved verses and journal to sync, and delete your account anytime from Profile.
